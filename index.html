Our Services

A Complete Sales & Marketing Solution

I help modular office, garden room and portable building companies attract potential customers, manage sales enquiries and turn more opportunities into completed sales.

Targeted Digital Advertising

I create and manage high-converting ad campaigns on Meta (Facebook & Instagram) to put your products in front of the right people in your target market.

  • Location & interest targeting
  • Compelling ad creatives
  • Ongoing optimisation

Enquiry Management & Sales Conversion

I personally handle incoming enquiries, respond to questions, and follow up with interested buyers to help move qualified leads towards a purchase.

  • Fast response times
  • Professional customer communication
  • Sales-focused follow-ups

Performance-Based Partnership

For the first 30 days, I handle the advertising management and sales conversion at no upfront service fee — you only pay for the ad spend I use. After that, if the sales are good and we decide to partner long-term, you'll be the one paying for the ad spend moving forward.

  • 30-day trial (you pay the ad cost)
  • Commission on completed sales
  • Your business handles delivery & fulfilment
